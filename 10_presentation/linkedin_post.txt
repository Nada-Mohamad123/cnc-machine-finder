We just finished a data project that started with a simple question: how does someone choose a CNC machine when they have a fixed budget?

The information exists, but it is spread over thousands of pages, written with mixed units, and most of the prices are estimates. So we collected it ourselves.

What we did:
- Scraped 3,955 machine pages from machinetoolindex.com with Python and BeautifulSoup
- Cleaned 5,213 raw columns into one comparable table (mm, kg, kW, rpm)
- Wrote 34 SQL queries on a SQLite database
- Built a Streamlit dashboard where you enter a budget and a minimum machine size and get a shortlist

Two things we found:
1. Machine type sets the price level. The median price goes from $200K for lathes to $950K for boring machines.
2. Size and weight follow price closely (rank correlation about 0.75). Spindle speed does not (about 0.34).

One caution that we put on every page of the dashboard: 95% of the prices we found are the website's own estimates, not quotes. They are good for shortlisting, but you still need a real quotation before buying.

We covered 6 of the website's 19 categories, so collecting the rest is our next step.
